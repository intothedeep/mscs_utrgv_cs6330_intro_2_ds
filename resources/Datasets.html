Feel free to use any data you like. You can scrape any websites to extract data as well. Here is the list of resources that might help you to find some data. Try to pick a dataset which has more than couple of features. When you are picking data, think about what kind of questions you are going to answer using the data.  What kind of story you want to extract from this data. What kind of relationship you can find between the features. What can you predict using this data.  What kind of groups/clusters you have in this data that you want to find.
