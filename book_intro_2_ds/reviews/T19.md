## Issues

- **I1 (implementation → developer), 10_01 left panel.** This is a slide-10 fidelity
  defect, not an AC miss: §7.3.6 requires the three colours only on the right panel, and that is met. (i) The observation (HH, 2 heads,
  slide 9) has the same colour as the equally rare TT, so slide 10's three parts
  (0.25 + 0.25 + 0) are not visible. (ii) Orange means "equally rare" on the left but
  "observed" on the right: one figure uses one colour for two meanings. (iii) The legend
  label "equally likely" on the 1-head bar is wrong. That bar is simply not part of the
  p-value. Fix: use the right panel's colour mapping (2 = observed, 0 = equally rare, no
  more-extreme bar), give the 1-head bar a neutral grey with no label or "not counted", and
  move the legend so it does not cover the 0.5 bar.
- **I2 (implementation → developer), 10_02 left title.** It prints the two-sided p as
  `0.050` (3 d.p.), while the annotations show 0.0234 + 0.0268. R11's boundary argument
  (p = 0.05 is not < 0.05) depends on this digit. Print `0.0502`.
- **I3 (reasoning → system-architect), 62 vs 63 contradiction in architecture.md.** R15
  says the book "unifies on 63" and footnotes 62. R13, §7.3.6 (`fig:pv-vitd` panel 2,
  `fig:pv-ci-test`) and the T19 AC demand the 62-centred intervals [55.4, 68.6] and
  [53.5, 70.5]. The developer followed the AC. As a result 10_03 panel 1 says "observed = 63",
  panel 2 says "observed 62", and 10_04 uses the 62 intervals, while the chapter text (R15)
  will say 63. The architect must pick one of two options:
  - **Option A, unify on 63** (R15's own decision). 95% ±2·SE = [56.4, 69.6], 99% ±2.576·SE = [54.5, 71.5].
    The figures are consistent with the chapter, but they depart from slide 15's printed
    "62 ± 2·3.3 = 55.4 to 68.6". Keep that line only as a recomputation footnote. The T19 AC numbers change, and T19 must re-run.
  - **Option B, keep the slide-faithful 62 intervals.** Add an in-figure annotation and a caption
    sentence naming the switch (slide 15 uses 62; slides 17-18 use 63; R15). The AC is unchanged.
  Reviewer lean: A (one story per figure). B is acceptable if it is annotated. Today only panel 2's title
  parenthetical "(slide 15, R13)" hints at the switch.
- **I4 (implementation → developer, minor), 10_03 labels.** The panel-1 title cites
  "slides 15, 17, 18", but the null simulation is slides 17-18 (slide 15 is the CI). The
  panel-2 x-label reads "population mean". Slide 15 (image36) plots the distribution of the
  *sample* mean around the true mean. Relabel it when I3 is applied.

## Answers to the three questions

- **(a) Unequal tails 0.0234/0.0268, p 0.0502: correct, keep it.** 155.7 is the slide's own
  mean (image33), and R12 needs it too. Recentring on 155.5 to force 0.025 each would
  contradict the slide and change R12. The mismatch comes from the slide's rounded
  interval [142, 169], and it is a finding to report. Only change: print 0.0502 (I2). The
  caption/T20 should say that the tails are unequal because the interval is rounded. 0.0502
  > 0.05 only reinforces R11's "fail to reject at the boundary".
- **(b) Both 62 and 63 in one figure: yes, confusing. Needs change (I3).** With nothing in
  the figure explaining the switch except a title parenthetical, a reader sees two "observed"
  values one panel apart. Either unify on 63 (option A) or annotate the switch explicitly
  (option B). This is the architect's call because the AC itself encodes 62.
- **(c) Uncorrected chi-square for 10_05: correct.** It is the test whose size is closest
  to nominal. The reviewer's check on the same seed gave uncorrected 4.6%, Yates 3.3%,
  Fisher 3.3%. Yates or Fisher would visibly undershoot slide 6's "5%" and teach the wrong
  lesson. It also matches R5/R6, which use uncorrected. The axis label already names the
  test. The caption should say that corrected tests are conservative (R4 uses Yates
  because that reproduces the slide).
