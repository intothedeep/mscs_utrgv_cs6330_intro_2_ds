## Module split

Justified. One module would be about 600 lines, over the 400-line hard review limit. The seam
follows responsibilities: distribution checks (07_01-05), transforms (07_06-10), and constants.
**ISSUE I4**: `_qq_positions` is defined identically in both figure modules. code.md forbids
duplicated logic, and it should live in one place (e.g. `deck7_values`). Also latent: in
`quantile_normalize`, the docstring says ties get the mean of their tied slots, but the code takes
the row mean at the mean-rank position. That is correct for 2-way ties (E4) and wrong for odd
k >= 3 ties: probe `[[1,1],[5,2],[5,3],[5,10]]` gives 4.0 where the rule gives
mean(3.5, 4, 7.5) = 5.0. No current data triggers it.
